**CurtainVerse** is a publishing platform designed for poets, authors, and dramatists to share their work in a space that emphasizes creativity and expression.  
The goal was to create a literary platform that feels like both a reading nook and a writer’s studio, offering tools to publish, browse, and engage with writing in multiple forms.  

---

## What is CurtainVerse?

CurtainVerse is built around the idea of providing writers with a stage that celebrates their words. Whether it’s poetry, short fiction, or dramatic scripts, the platform provides a structured and visually expressive environment for publishing and discovering creative works.  

### Core Capabilities
- Write, edit, and publish works across poetry, prose, and drama  
- Create personalized author profiles with bio and genre tags  
- Filter and browse content by type, genre, and date  
- Discover and engage with works published by other authors  

---

## Tech Stack & Features

- **React.js** – component-based frontend development  
- **Tailwind CSS** – responsive and scalable design system  
- **Sanity.io** – headless CMS with schema-driven content management  
- **Portable Text & Rich Editor** – flexible formatting for creative writing  
- **GROQ Queries** – efficient data fetching and filtering  
- **Live Previews** – instant feedback while drafting content  
- **Dynamic Routing** – SEO-friendly URLs for posts and author pages  
- **Author Profiles** – dedicated profile pages linking to works  
- **Content Filtering** – by category, type, and mood  

---

## Design & User Experience

CurtainVerse is designed to feel literary, timeless, and user-friendly. The emphasis is on typography, spacious layouts, and flow, ensuring the content itself takes center stage.  

- Clean, content-focused layouts with generous white space  
- Fully responsive across mobile, tablet, and desktop  
- Subtle animations and transitions to maintain an elegant feel  
- Carefully chosen typography to reinforce a literary tone  
- Clear hierarchy so every published work feels showcased  

---

## Development Highlights

- Structured around **Sanity’s schema-based backend** to support diverse post types (poem, story, play)  
- Implemented **GROQ-based filtering** for genre and type-specific browsing  
- Built modular components such as **PostCard**, **ProfilePage**, and **EditorView**  
- Linked author profiles with their works through structured content relationships  
- Enabled synchronized draft and preview modes for real-time editing  
- Added custom logic for draft vs. published states to prepare for role-based permissions
