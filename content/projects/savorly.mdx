**Savorly** is a recipe discovery and meal planning platform created for home cooks, food enthusiasts, and busy planners who want a personalized and organized way to manage their meals.  
It combines clean design, structured data, and smart filters to deliver a modern cooking companion.  

---

## What is Savorly?

Savorly allows users to browse recipes, save favorites, and generate shopping lists with ease. Whether the goal is to cook something quick, plan a week’s meals, or discover plant-based options, the app adapts to the user’s preferences.  

### Core Capabilities
- Discover recipes by mood, ingredients, or dietary preferences  
- Save meals into a personal, digital cookbook  
- Generate auto-formatted shopping lists from selected recipes  
- Plan meals across days or weeks with a structured recipe catalog  

---

## Tech Stack & Features

- **React.js** – modular and dynamic frontend  
- **Tailwind CSS** – responsive design and utility-first styling  
- **Sanity.io** – content management for recipes, tags, and filters  
- **GROQ Queries** – fast and customizable recipe fetching  
- **Live Previews** – edit and update content in real-time  
- **Search & Filter** – recipe discovery by mood, diet, or cooking time  
- **Dynamic Routing** – clean and SEO-friendly recipe pages  
- **Local Storage Cookbook** – offline-ready saving without login  

---

## Design & UX Principles

Savorly is designed to function as both a **modern cookbook** and a **digital kitchen assistant**. The interface emphasizes clarity, organization, and ease of use, making meal planning approachable and enjoyable.  

- Recipe-focused layouts with structured content hierarchy  
- Fully responsive across mobile, tablet, and desktop  
- Large, high-quality images with smooth transitions  
- Ingredient lists and instructions formatted for readability  
- Tag-based categorization for browsing by themes and diets  

---

## Development Highlights

- Built on Sanity’s schema system for flexible recipe modeling  
- Implemented GROQ filters for mood- and diet-based recommendations  
- Created reusable UI components such as **RecipeCard**, **FilterBar**, and **CookbookGrid**  
- Added auto-generated shopping lists based on recipe metadata  
- Integrated local storage for quick bookmarking and retrieval of saved recipes  
- Developed SEO-friendly route slugs for recipe detail pages
