**FinApt** is a personal finance management platform designed to simplify the way users track and manage their money.  
It focuses on making expense tracking and budgeting more intuitive by combining clean design with intelligent features such as receipt scanning and personalized alerts.  

---

## What is FinApt?

FinApt enables users to keep a clear record of their expenses, analyze spending patterns, and stay informed about their financial habits. Instead of manually sorting through spreadsheets or paper receipts, the app provides a structured and automated approach to personal finance.  

### Core Capabilities
- Track expenses through a user-friendly dashboard  
- Scan receipts using **Gemini AI** for instant digitization and categorization  
- Identify spending trends and gain insights over time  
- Receive notifications when monthly spending limits are close to being exceeded  
- Secure authentication and user management with **Clerk**  

---

## Tech Stack & Features

- **Next.js** – application framework for fast and scalable development  
- **Tailwind CSS** – responsive styling and consistent design system  
- **Clerk** – authentication and session management  
- **Gemini AI** – intelligent receipt scanning and categorization  
- **Prisma** – database ORM for structured and reliable data handling  
- **Shadcn UI** – modern, accessible user interface components  
- **Ingest** – data logging and structured event tracking  

---

## Design & User Experience

The design of FinApt prioritizes clarity, simplicity, and ease of use. Users can quickly review their financial position without being overwhelmed by unnecessary complexity.  

- Minimalist dashboards with clear expense breakdowns  
- Fully responsive layout optimized for mobile and desktop  
- Smooth interface with intuitive navigation across key sections  
- Reusable, consistent components to ensure scalability and maintainability  

---

## Development Highlights

- Developed a **receipt scanning feature** powered by Gemini AI to digitize and categorize expenditures automatically  
- Integrated a **personalized notification system** that alerts users when their spending approaches defined limits  
- Built secure authentication and account management using Clerk  
- Designed and implemented reusable components such as **ExpenseCard**, **ReceiptUploader**, and **BudgetAlert**  
- Structured the database with Prisma to ensure efficiency and reliability in handling financial data
