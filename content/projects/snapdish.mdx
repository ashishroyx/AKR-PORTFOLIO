**SnapDish** is an AI-integrated culinary platform that redefines the cooking experience. By combining **TheMealDB API**, **Strapi CMS**, and **Google Gemini AI**, SnapDish offers an "Infinite Cookbook" experience—bridging the gap between what's in your pantry and what's for dinner.

---

## The Concept: An Infinite Cookbook

Most recipe apps are limited by their database. SnapDish breaks this barrier. If a user wants a dish that doesn't exist in the traditional records, the platform leverages **Generative AI** to construct a professional-grade recipe on the fly. Coupled with a **Visual Pantry Scanner**, it turns your smartphone camera into a culinary expert.

### Core Features
- **Visual Pantry Scanner** – Uses **Gemini AI Vision** to analyze photos of ingredients, instantly identifying items and populating a digital pantry.
- **AI Ingredient Matching** – Intelligent suggestion engine that maps your current pantry inventory to relevant, high-quality recipes.
- **Infinite Recipe Generation** – When a search falls outside the database, **Gemini AI** generates complete recipes including step-by-step instructions and nutritional data.
- **Tiered Subscription Logic** – Implemented a "Freemium" model with daily AI usage quotas for free users and unlimited access for Pro-tier subscribers.
- **Secure Authentication** – Managed by **Clerk**, providing seamless social logins and persistent user session management.

---

## Tech Stack

- **Framework**: Next.js 15+ (App Router & Edge Functions)
- **AI Engine**: Google Generative AI (Gemini Pro Vision)
- **Content Management**: Strapi (Headless CMS for curated data)
- **Database**: Neon DB (Serverless PostgreSQL)
- **External API**: TheMealDB for global recipe data
- **Authentication**: Clerk (Identity & User Profiles)
- **Styling**: Tailwind CSS & Shadcn UI

---

## Design & Responsiveness

SnapDish features a **Cinematic UI** specifically built for high-utility environments like kitchens. The design is mobile-first, recognizing that most users will interact with the app while standing at a counter or holding a phone.

- **Mobile-First UX**: Optimized touch targets and "cook-mode" layouts for iPad and smartphone users.
- **Dynamic Content Loading**: Smooth, non-blocking UI transitions when fetching AI-generated content or scanning images.
- **Responsive Layouts**: A fluid grid system that scales from a single-column mobile view to a rich, multi-panel desktop dashboard.

---

## Engineering Highlights

- **Multi-Modal AI Integration**: Successfully implemented **Gemini Pro Vision** to handle image-to-text processing for the pantry scanner.
- **Hybrid Data Strategy**: Combined static data from **TheMealDB** and **Strapi** with dynamic, AI-generated content to ensure the app never returns an "empty" search result.
- **Subscription Middleware**: Developed custom logic to track and restrict AI API calls based on user tier, preventing resource over-usage while maintaining a smooth UX.
- **Headless CMS Architecture**: Used **Strapi** to allow for easy updates to curated content without requiring redeployments of the frontend.
