**Finapt** is a sophisticated personal finance management platform designed to help users track, analyze, and optimize their financial health with surgical precision. By integrating **Next.js 16** and **Google Gemini AI**, it transforms the tedious chore of manual expense tracking into a seamless, automated experience.

---

## What is Finapt?

Finapt was built to solve the fragmentation in personal finance. Instead of static spreadsheets, it provides a dynamic ecosystem where financial data is alive and actionable. Whether it's scanning a physical bill or forecasting a monthly budget, the platform acts as an intelligent co-pilot for your money.

### Core Features
- **AI Smart Receipt Scanning** – Integrated with **Google Gemini AI** to instantly scan, read, and categorize physical bills or digital receipts, eliminating manual data entry.
- **Intelligent Financial Advisor** – Contextual advice and data-driven budget forecasting powered by the **Gemini API**.
- **Automated Workflows** – Powered by **Inngest**, handling the heavy lifting of recurring transactions and scheduled financial syncs in the background.
- **Advanced Analytics** – Dynamic spending charts and income distribution graphs built with **Recharts** for deep visual insights.
- **Bulletproof Data** – Type-safe database operations using **Prisma ORM** connected to a high-performance **Supabase (PostgreSQL)** cluster.

---

## Tech Stack

- **Framework**: Next.js 16 (Turbopack)
- **Intelligence**: Google Generative AI (Gemini) for OCR & Financial Logic
- **Database & ORM**: Prisma with Supabase PostgreSQL
- **Automation**: Inngest (Event-driven background functions)
- **Authentication**: Clerk (Secure User Management)
- **Styling**: Tailwind CSS, Shadcn UI, and Lucide React
- **Visualization**: Recharts

---

## Design & Responsiveness

Finapt features a sleek, modern UI built with **Shadcn UI**, **Radix UI**, and **Vaul** drawers. The design philosophy focuses on "Mobile-First" usability, ensuring that the financial dashboard is as powerful on a smartphone as it is on a desktop.

- **Fully Responsive Architecture**: Custom-engineered layouts that adapt seamlessly to mobile, tablet, and desktop screens.
- **Cinematic UI**: A premium dark-mode aesthetic utilizing high-fidelity components for a professional fintech feel.
- **Accessible Navigation**: Intuitive user flows designed to make complex financial data easy to digest at a glance.

---

## Development Highlights

- **AI Integration**: Successfully implemented **Gemini AI** for high-accuracy OCR, allowing the app to "understand" receipt data rather than just store it.
- **Structured Data**: Leveraged **Prisma** to ensure 100% data integrity across complex financial relationships and transaction histories.
- **Component-Driven Development**: Built a library of reusable, accessible components using **Shadcn UI** to maintain design consistency.
- **Communication Layer**: Integrated **React Email** to provide users with automated, beautifully designed financial summaries and alerts.
